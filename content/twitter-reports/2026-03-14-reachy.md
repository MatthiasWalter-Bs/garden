## NVIDIA Partnership & Embodied AI

The NVIDIA partnership is the biggest story this week. Multiple major announcements at #NVIDIAGTC:

- **ASUS + HuggingFace + NVIDIA collaboration**: Reachy Mini + Ascent GX10 powered by NVIDIA GB10 Grace Blackwell. Physical AI that can see, hear, and think. Live demo at Booth #421.  
  *Source: @ASUSUSA*

- **Dell partnership**: Dell teaming up with Pollen Robotics, Hugging Face, and NVIDIA to support hands-on, open learning for builders of all ages. Multiple Dell employees sharing this.  
  *Source: @Robert_Mc2, @jhmachinski*

- **Embodied AI Hackathon**: NVIDIA Robotics promoting Reachy Mini as a platform for personal AI assistants using NVIDIA Jetson Orin and open-source models.  
  *Source: @NVIDIARobotics (quoted by @SuperTechGod)*

- **DGX Spark integration**: Japanese coverage on "NVIDIA's desktop AI robot" - DGX Spark × Reachy Mini and how it changes the future of work.  
  *Source: @shirakabado*
